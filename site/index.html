Pig needs a bigger pen. You need a good puzzle.

A puzzle game about building the biggest pen you can with a limited number of fence pieces. How big can you build?

Get it on the App Store

iPhone · iOS 17 and later

Daily puzzles and the first world for free. One purchase unlocks the whole game.

How to play

Each puzzle gives you a new board, a set number of fences, and one simple goal: use every piece and enclose as much space as possible. Tap to place fences, plan your route, and watch out for obstacles, bonuses, and other surprises along the way.

Twelve worlds

A meadow, a thicket, a mountain, a clockwork city, the stars, a cavern, a carnival, dunes, a cove, tundra, a fen and the heights above the weather. Nine puzzles each — 108 in all, every one of them solvable with the pieces it gives you.

A new puzzle every morning

One board a day, the same board for everybody, with a clock on it and a run of days behind it. Two years of them are written already. The archive keeps every morning there has been.
