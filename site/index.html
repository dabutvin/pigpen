Pigpen

A grid, a pig, and a strict number of fence pieces. Pen the pig in — and pen in as much mud as you can while you are at it.

Coming to the App Store

iPhone · iOS 17 and later

Windfall Orchard, held — twelve pieces round 27 tiles and two apples, which is the best pen that map has in it, and why the ground has gone rainbow.

How it goes

Tap a tile to wall it off, or press and drag to lay a whole run at once. The pig walks up, down, left and right, never diagonally, and when you let it go it tries every route it has. One gap that reaches the edge of the map and it walks out.

Holding the pig is only half of it. A pen is scored on the ground it shuts in, so the question is never can I trap it but how much mud can I trap it with — and your budget of fence pieces is the same either way. Water walls a pen for free. An apple inside is worth five tiles, a skull costs five, and neither takes a fence, so a wall that meets one has to swallow it or step in beside it.

Twelve worlds

A meadow, a thicket, a mountain, a clockwork city, the stars, a cavern, a carnival, dunes, a cove, tundra, a fen and the heights above the weather. Nine puzzles each — 108 in all, every one of them solvable with the pieces it gives you.

A boss at the end of every world

The last field of a world stands a second animal on it and changes a rule: pen them together, pen them apart, pen them evenly. A deer in the meadow, a boar in the thicket, a wyrm on the mountain, a rat king in the city.

A new puzzle every morning

One board a day, the same board for everybody, with a clock on it and a run of days behind it. Two years of them are written already. The archive keeps every morning you have held.

A story, told in films

Every world opens on one, briefs you before its boss, and sends you off when its last pen holds. Skip any of them; watch the whole reel end to end whenever you like.

Questions, bugs, ideas

The support page has the common ones on it, and there is an address behind it that reaches a person: support@pigpen.app.

The game is built in the open, so a bug can be reported where it will be fixed: github.com/dabutvin/pigpen/issues.
