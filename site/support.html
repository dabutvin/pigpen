Get in touch

Something broken, something confusing, or an idea for the game — write, and you will get a person rather than a form. Say which iPhone you are on and which version of Pigpen you are running; the version number is behind the gear on the title screen, at the top of the settings sheet.

support@pigpen.app

Replies usually come within a few days.

Common questions

How do I play?

Tap a tile to lay a fence on it; press and drag to lay a whole run at once, and start a drag on a fence to tear out everything you drag over. Shut the pig in with the pieces the level gives you, and shut in as much mud as you can while you are at it. Water walls the pen for free. When you let the pig go it tries every route, and a single gap to the edge of the map is an escape.

I am stuck on a level.

Every level in the game has been solved with the pieces it gives you. Undo walks back a press at a time, and clearing the field is a press like any other — one undo brings the whole thing back. A pen that holds is worth more the more ground it takes, so a first pass that merely holds is a fine place to start.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off, or move the hour?

Behind the gear on the title screen, under Daily puzzle reminder. If the switch is on and nothing comes through, notifications for Pigpen have been turned off for the whole app in iPhone Settings; the card says so and gives you the button that opens that door.

How do I stop the game counting anything?

Behind the gear, under Privacy: turn Anonymous usage off and nothing more is sent. What is counted, and what is not, is written out in full in the privacy policy.

How do I start over?

Behind the gear, under Game data: Clear all game data forgets every star, every level you have opened and every daily puzzle you have held. You are asked first, and it cannot be undone. The button greys out when there is nothing saved to clear.

Can I watch the cut scenes again?

Yes — behind the gear, under Cut scenes: every film in the game end to end, in the order the journey meets them, including worlds you have not reached yet.

Does my progress follow me to another phone?

No. Everything the game remembers is kept on the phone you played it on. There is no account to make and nothing to sign in to.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. It is a game about a pig getting into and out of mud: no violence, no chat, and no links out to anywhere except this page and the privacy policy.
